ABOUT US

Travel Made Simple

Our Travel Booking Website helps users discover destinations, compare packages and create a convenient travel plan.

Why choose our platform?

This college mini project demonstrates how HTML, CSS and JavaScript can be combined to create an interactive travel booking experience without a backend.

Users can explore destinations, filter packages, calculate prices, submit bookings and view their booking history directly in the browser.
